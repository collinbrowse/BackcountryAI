Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

BackcountryAI does not collect any data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your questions, Scout's answers, saved answers, chat history and anything you enter in Field mode (patient notes, vitals) are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Answers are written on the iPhone itself, by Apple's built-in model or, if you download the optional "Better answers" model (Qwen 3.5 4B, an open model from Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team), by that model. Both run entirely on your iPhone: your questions are not sent to Apple, Alibaba or anyone else. You can delete chats and saved answers in the app, and deleting the app removes everything.

Network use

The app uses the network only to download its offline library and, if you choose it, the optional "Better answers" model. Both are hosted by Apple. Those downloads do not include your questions or any personal information. Apple may log standard download requests (such as an IP address) under its own privacy policy. After the downloads, the app works in Airplane Mode.

No accounts, analytics or ads

There is no sign-in. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
