Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

BackcountryAI does not collect any data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your questions, Scout's answers, saved answers, chat history and anything you enter in Field mode (patient notes, vitals) are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Answers are written by Apple's on-device model; your questions do not leave the phone. You can delete chats and saved answers in the app, and deleting the app removes everything.

Network use

The app uses the network only to download its offline library, which is hosted by Apple, and, if you choose it in the app's Lab screen, an optional answer model hosted by Hugging Face. Those downloads do not include your questions or any personal information. Apple and Hugging Face may log standard download requests (such as an IP address) under their own privacy policies.

No accounts, analytics or ads

There is no sign-in. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
