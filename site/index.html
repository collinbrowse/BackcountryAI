BackcountryAI

A field guide you can talk to, with no signal.

Describe what's happening in your own words. Scout finds the right passages in a 277,000-article survival, first aid and repair library stored on your iPhone, and writes a step-by-step answer on the phone itself. Every answer shows its sources.

  • Works in Airplane Mode once the library is downloaded
  • Optional "Better answers": a larger on-device model for more specific answers
  • SOS guidance and one-tap emergency topics
  • Field mode: patient assessment, vitals log and SOAP note
  • No account, no tracking, no data collected

BackcountryAI is a reference, not a substitute for professional medical care, training or emergency services. In an emergency, call for help first.
