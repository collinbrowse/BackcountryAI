Frequently Asked Questions

Scout says the AI isn't available

Written answers need Apple Intelligence, including when you use Better answers. Turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri while you have Wi-Fi, and wait for its model to finish downloading. On iPhones that can't run Apple Intelligence you can still search and read the whole library and use Field mode.

The library won't download

The Essentials library is about 0.8 GB and needs about 2.7 GB free while it installs. Use Wi-Fi, keep the app open until it finishes, and check free space in Settings › General › iPhone Storage.

What is "Better answers"?

An optional larger AI model (Qwen 3.5 4B) that writes Scout's answers in place of Apple's built-in model. It follows the details of your situation more closely and gives more specific steps. It runs entirely on your iPhone and works with no signal. The trade-offs: a 2.3 GB download, answers take about 10 seconds instead of about 3, and each answer uses more battery. Get it from the Better answers card on the Offline library screen.

Better answers won't download

Use Wi-Fi and keep about 4.7 GB free while it installs (it takes 2.4 GB once installed). It needs an iPhone that runs Apple Intelligence. If it says it isn't available from the App Store yet, update the app and try again.

Answers are slow or my iPhone gets warm

That's the larger model at work. On the Better answers card, switch from Better to Faster to go back to Apple's built-in model; the download stays on your iPhone so you can switch back. The first answer after opening the app can take a few extra seconds while the model loads.

Removing Better answers

Open the Better answers card, tap How it works, then Remove better answers from this iPhone. Scout goes back to Apple's built-in model, and you get the 2.4 GB back.

How can I make sure BackcountryAI will work before I leave service?

Open the checklist from the pill at the top left of the Scout tab. Then turn on Airplane Mode and ask a question: if you get an answer, you're set.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored on your iPhone. Delete chats from Chat history, remove saved answers from the Saved tab, or delete the app to remove it all.

A wrong or unclear answer

Tap the sources under the answer to read the original articles, and email us the question so we can improve the library.
