Scout says the AI isn't available

Written answers use Apple Intelligence. Turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri while you have Wi-Fi, and wait for its model to finish downloading. On iPhones that can't run Apple Intelligence you can still search and read the whole library and use Field mode.

The library won't download

The Essentials library is about 0.8 GB and needs about 2.7 GB free while it installs. Use Wi-Fi, keep the app open until it finishes, and check free space in Settings › General › iPhone Storage.

Check that you're ready before a trip

Open the checklist from the pill at the top left of the Scout tab. Then turn on Airplane Mode and ask a question: if you get an answer, you're set.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored on your iPhone. Delete chats from Chat history, remove saved answers from the Saved tab, or delete the app to remove it all.

A wrong or unclear answer

Tap the sources under the answer to read the original articles, and email us the question so we can improve the library.
