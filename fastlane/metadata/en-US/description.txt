BackcountryAI is a field guide you can talk to, and it works with no signal.

Describe what's happening in your own words: "My friend rolled their ankle six miles from the trailhead." Scout, the app's guide, finds the right passages in a library stored on your iPhone and writes a clear, step-by-step answer on the phone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere, so it works in Airplane Mode, in a canyon, or a week from the nearest cell tower.

ANSWERS YOU CAN CHECK
• Every answer lists the articles it came from. Tap a citation to read the original.
• Sources include the National Park Service, National Weather Service, CDC, FEMA, U.S. Army survival, first aid and cold-weather manuals, MedlinePlus, and outdoors, vehicle, bike and home-repair Q&A.
• When something isn't in the library, Scout says so.

A LIBRARY THAT LIVES ON YOUR PHONE
• 277,000 articles in one download (about 0.8 GB). Get it once on Wi-Fi before you leave.
• Browse by topic: wilderness first aid, weather and environment, lost and rescue, camp skills, wildlife, vehicles.
• Search the whole library instantly, with no AI and almost no battery.

BUILT FOR EMERGENCIES
• SOS on every chat screen: how to reach help first, then what to do while you wait.
• One-tap guides for heavy bleeding, hypothermia, burns, fractures, allergic reactions, snakebite and heat illness.
• Field mode: a Wilderness First Responder-style patient assessment, a vitals log and a SOAP note you can hand to rescuers, plus quick reference cards.

MADE FOR THE BACKCOUNTRY
• Private by design: no account, no tracking, no data collected.
• Battery-aware: see what each answer cost, and save answers to reread for free.
• Chats save automatically on your phone, and you can delete them any time.
• A "Trail ready" checklist confirms everything works offline before you go.

REQUIREMENTS
Scout's written answers use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. On iPhones without it, you can still search and read the entire library and use Field mode.

IMPORTANT
BackcountryAI is a reference, not a substitute for professional medical care, training or emergency services. In an emergency, call for help first.
